## Validation Milestone

Competition Framework v1 was validated through three independent examples:

1. A standings-based mini group.
2. A knockout invitational.
3. An 8-team domestic cup prototype.

The domestic cup prototype is especially important because it represents the first recognizable non-World-Cup competition built entirely from reusable framework components.

## Status

Competition Framework v1 is complete.

Future framework work should be driven by concrete application needs rather than abstract expansion.
