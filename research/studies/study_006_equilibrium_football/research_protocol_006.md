# Research Protocol 007

## Competitive Resolution

### An Empirical Investigation of How Competitive Football Matches Are Resolved

---

# Protocol Status

Status: Draft

---

# 1. Motivation

Studies 004–006 established that:

- one-goal football represents a major football population,
- draw football represents a distinct football population,
- draw football itself separates into scoreless and scoring equilibrium.

These studies naturally motivate a broader question.

Rather than studying football populations independently, the present study investigates how competitive matches become resolved.

---

# 2. Primary Research Question

> What distinguishes competitive matches that end level from competitive matches that produce a narrow winner?

9. Success Criteria

The study succeeds if it identifies measurable differences between draw football and one-goal football that generate new hypotheses about competitive football.

Prediction improvement is not required.
