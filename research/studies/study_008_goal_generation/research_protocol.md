# Research Protocol 008

# Goal Generation

### An Empirical Investigation of Scoreline Realization

---

# Protocol Status

Draft

Purpose:

Transition Computational Football from descriptive studies toward explanatory football mechanisms.

---

# Motivation

Previous Observatory studies successfully characterized several football populations.

These studies answered questions such as:

- What is one-goal football?
- What is draw football?
- How does competitive football resolve?

However, these studies primarily described football outcomes.

The present study investigates the process connecting pre-match expectations to realized scorelines.

---

# Primary Research Question

> How are football scorelines generated from pre-match expectations?

---

# Secondary Research Questions

## RQ1

Why do similar expected-goal values produce different scorelines?

---

## RQ2

Which scoreline discrepancies consistently appear in generated football?

---

## RQ3

Which scoreline discrepancies appear systematic rather than random?

---

## RQ4

Can these discrepancies be explained by observable football mechanisms?

---

## RQ5

Which candidate mechanisms appear sufficiently supported to justify research prototypes?

---

Historical football

Production match generator

Football Gap Report

Previous Observatory studies

# Planned Analyses

## Analysis 1

Football Gap Review

Re-examine all remaining scoreline discrepancies.

---

## Analysis 2

Mechanism Candidates

For each discrepancy:

Possible football explanation.

Examples:

- competitive resolution
- defensive separation
- mutual scoring
- offensive escalation

---

## Analysis 3

Evidence Mapping

Map every candidate mechanism back to:

Studies

Knowledge Base

Football Gap Report

---

## Analysis 4

Prototype Readiness

Determine which mechanism has sufficient evidence to become Prototype 002.

---

# Success Criteria

The study succeeds if it identifies one football mechanism that:

- is empirically supported,
- possesses a plausible implementation,
- can be benchmarked independently,
- can be isolated behind the research seam.

---

# Non-Goals

The study will not:

implement football mechanisms,

modify production code,

or tune scoreline frequencies directly.

Its purpose is explanatory.

---

# Deliverables

Updated Football Gap interpretation.

Mechanism ranking.

Prototype recommendation.

Updated Football Knowledge Base.

Prototype 002 recommendation.

---

# Decision Rules

Candidate mechanisms must satisfy:

Scientific support.

Engineering feasibility.

Architectural compatibility.

Benchmarkability.

If no mechanism satisfies these criteria:

Prototype 002 remains postponed.

This is considered a successful scientific outcome.

---

# Guiding Principle

The objective is no longer to describe football.

The objective is to explain football well enough that the simulator can eventually learn from it.
