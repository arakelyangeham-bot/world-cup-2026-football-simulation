# Mechanism Audit

## Study 008 — Goal Generation

### Transition from Descriptive Football to Explanatory Football

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this audit is to determine which football mechanism has accumulated sufficient empirical support to justify implementation inside the Research Match Engine.

This document does not propose algorithms.

Instead, it evaluates candidate football mechanisms using evidence accumulated from previous Observatory studies.

Only mechanisms judged sufficiently mature should proceed to Research Prototype 002.

---

# Candidate Mechanism 1

## Competitive Resolution

### Description

Competitive football appears to resolve through multiple observable populations, primarily:

- Draw football
- One-goal football

The mechanism attempts to explain how similarly balanced matches resolve into these different outcomes.

### Supporting Evidence

- Study 004
- Study 005
- Study 006
- Study 007
- Football Gap Report

### Evidence Strength

★★★★☆

Multiple independent studies support the existence of the phenomenon.

However, the underlying causal process remains unknown.

### Engineering Readiness

Medium

The phenomenon is well documented.

The implementation mechanism is not yet obvious.

### Recommendation

Continue investigation.

Do not implement yet.

# Candidate Mechanism 2

## Low-Score Equilibrium

### Description

Certain matches appear to remain trapped in low-scoring equilibrium.

This may explain persistent 0–0 and 1–1 behaviour.

### Supporting Evidence

- Study 005
- Study 006
- Dixon–Coles calibration research

### Evidence Strength

★★★☆☆

The descriptive evidence is good.

Mechanistic evidence remains limited.

### Engineering Readiness

Low

Current evidence is insufficient to justify scoreline adjustments.

### Recommendation

Requires additional investigation.

---

# Candidate Mechanism 3

## Goal Dependence

### Description

Goals may not be generated independently.

Scoring by one team may alter the probability of subsequent scoring.

### Supporting Evidence

- Dixon–Coles research
- Football Gap Report
- Historical literature

### Evidence Strength

★★★★★

Strong support from both literature and project benchmarks.

### Engineering Readiness

High

Existing production engine already contains limited Dixon–Coles dependence.

Future work should investigate richer dependence structures rather than replacing the current implementation.

### Recommendation

Candidate for future prototype refinement.

---

---

# Overall Assessment

Current Observatory studies successfully identified several plausible football mechanisms.

However, most remain descriptive rather than explanatory.

Only Goal Dependence currently possesses:

- empirical support,
- existing implementation precedent,
- clear engineering interpretation.

The remaining mechanisms require additional explanatory research before implementation.

---

# Prototype Recommendation

Prototype 002 should **not** attempt to implement a completely new football mechanism.

Instead, it should investigate whether the existing goal-dependence framework can be extended using evidence generated by the Football Observatory.

This provides the smallest possible step from empirical football research toward research-based scoreline generation.

# Decision

No new football mechanism has yet accumulated sufficient explanatory evidence to justify direct scoreline modification.

Prototype 002 should therefore investigate extensions to existing goal-dependence modeling rather than introducing entirely new scoreline heuristics.

This maintains the project's guiding principle:

Historical football should determine engineering decisions.
