# Competition Research Program

## Mission

Investigate how competition structure influences football outcomes.

The primary objective is to determine how different competition formats reward strong teams, produce upsets, and influence tournament fairness.

# Key Findings

- League competitions consistently identify stronger champions than knockout competitions.
- Production Football Model v1 strengthens competitive discrimination while preserving the qualitative conclusions observed under the synthetic model.
