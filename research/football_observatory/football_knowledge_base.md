# Football Knowledge Base

## Version 1.0

### Computational Football Research

---

# Purpose

The Football Knowledge Base is the authoritative collection of empirical football knowledge generated by the Football Observatory.

It is **not** a study.

It is **not** a review.

It is the bridge between football research and football engineering.

Only knowledge recorded here may motivate changes to the research match generator.

---

# Knowledge Status Levels

Every knowledge item belongs to one of four stages.

## Exploratory

Observed in one study.

Requires confirmation.

---

## Supported

Observed independently in multiple studies.

Consistent evidence.

Suitable for research prototypes.

---

## Validated

Research prototypes support the finding.

Benchmark evidence is positive.

Candidate for production.

---

## Production

The knowledge has been incorporated into the production football engine.

# Promotion Rules

Knowledge may advance only through evidence.

Exploratory
→ Supported

Requires:

- independent replication

Supported
→ Validated

Requires:

- successful research prototype
- benchmark improvement

Validated
→ Production

Requires:

- production benchmark
- no regression
- architecture review

---

# Guiding Principle

Football knowledge should accumulate gradually.

The match generator should learn only from knowledge that has progressed through the complete research lifecycle.

Historical football determines the research agenda.

Research determines football knowledge.

Football knowledge determines engineering.

Engineering determines the simulator.
