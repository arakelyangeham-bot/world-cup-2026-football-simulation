# Version 1 Architecture

## Purpose

The World Cup 2026 Data Science Project is a football intelligence,
prediction, and simulation platform.

Its purpose is not merely to predict football matches.

Its purpose is to transform football evidence into simulated football
competitions through a sequence of well-defined, independently
validated layers.

Each layer has a single responsibility.

Research is conducted independently from production simulation.

Production components are promoted only after passing explicit
validation studies.

---

# Guiding Philosophy

Version 1 follows several architectural principles.

1. Football meaning precedes model complexity.

Every feature should represent a meaningful football concept rather
than merely improving statistical fit.

2. Prediction and realization are separate problems.

Predicting expected goals and generating a scoreline are distinct
responsibilities.

3. Research and production are separate concerns.

Research discovers models.

Production consumes validated artifacts.

4. Time validity is part of correctness.

Every prediction should use only information available before the
prediction date.

5. Negative research results are valuable.

Rejected ideas remain documented so they are not repeatedly
rediscovered.

---

# Conceptual Architecture

Version 1 transforms football information through the following layers.

Evidence
    ↓
Football Intelligence
    ↓
Team Representation
    ↓
Repository
    ↓
Match Observation
    ↓
Goal Prediction
    ↓
Football Realization
    ↓
Competition Simulation
    ↓
Simulation Results

Each layer has a clearly defined responsibility.

---

# Layer Descriptions

## Evidence

Evidence represents raw football information.

Examples include

- player statistics
- historical match results
- competition membership
- ClubElo histories
- FIFA rankings

Evidence is never consumed directly by production prediction.

---

## Football Intelligence

Football intelligence converts evidence into player and team
representations.

Player intelligence is responsible for estimating football ability,
roles, uncertainty, and evidence quality.

---

## Team Representation

A team representation summarizes football ability through player
aggregation.

Current production representations include

- attack
- midfield
- defense
- goalkeeper
- attack depth
- squad quality
- evidence quality

Representations intentionally exclude external rating priors.

---

## Repository

Repositories provide validated representations for a defined football
population.

Repositories do not perform prediction.

Repositories do not perform feature engineering.

Repositories are stable data products.

---

## Match Observation

A match observation combines

- home representation
- away representation
- external rating priors
- fixture context
- prediction date

The observation is the complete input to the prediction model.

---

## Goal Prediction

Goal prediction converts a match observation into

λ_home

λ_away

Prediction models never generate scorelines directly.

---

## Football Realization

Football realization transforms expected goals into one stochastic
football outcome.

Current realization uses the Dixon–Coles hierarchical goal sampler.

---

## Competition Simulation

Competition infrastructure organizes match realizations according to
league or tournament rules.

Competition logic is independent of prediction models.

---

# Production Architecture

The production club prediction route is

Production Repository
        +
Prediction-date ClubElo
        ↓
Live Match Observation
        ↓
Integrated Club Goal Model v1
        ↓
Expected Goals
        ↓
Dixon–Coles Sampler
        ↓
Match Result
        ↓
Competition Engine

---

# National-Team Architecture

National-team simulation currently follows an independent production
path.

National Repository
        +
FIFA Rating Prior
        ↓
National Goal Model
        ↓
Expected Goals
        ↓
Shared Scoreline Sampler

This separation reflects differences in available evidence and model
training rather than architectural inconsistency.

---

# Research Architecture

Research surrounds production rather than replacing it.

Research Question
        ↓
Study
        ↓
Benchmark
        ↓
Evidence
        ↓
Decision

Successful studies produce production artifacts.

Negative studies remain part of the permanent evidence base.

---

# Stable Version 1 Interfaces

Version 1 considers the following interfaces stable.

- Team Representation
- Repository schema
- Match Observation
- Goal Predictor
- Expected Goals interface
- Scoreline sampler interface
- Competition interface

These interfaces may support future models without changing downstream
components.

---

# Scope of Version 1

Version 1 provides

- player-derived football intelligence
- production club goal prediction
- production national-team prediction
- calendar-aware competition simulation
- model-agnostic competition infrastructure
- reproducible research studies

Version 1 does not yet include

- dynamic live lineup prediction
- realistic fixture congestion
- transfer windows
- injury modeling
- tactical adaptation
- in-match state simulation

These remain future research directions.

---

# Version 1 Status

Version 1 establishes a complete scoreline-first football simulation
platform.

Research components are promoted only through explicit validation.

Prediction, realization, and competition simulation remain independent
architectural layers.
